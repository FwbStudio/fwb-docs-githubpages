# Pizza Shop

### 🍕 Pizza Shop – Deliver Slices, Build a Brand

Bring a complete pizza restaurant job to your city with crafting stations, delivery flow, stock control, and management tools. Pizza Shop mirrors the FWB food-business formula — tuned for pizzeria RP from kitchen to counter.

Supports **ESX**, **QBCore**, and **Qbox** via FS Bridge.

#### ⚙️ Key Features

* **Pizza Crafting** — Prep dough, toppings, and finished pies through configured crafting stations.
* **Stock & Pricing** — Boss menus for price changes, stock levels, and business management.
* **Customer Billing** — Charge players at the counter with configurable payment flow.
* **Target Integration** — Works with ox_target or marker-based interaction zones.
* **Integration Ready** — Pairs with other FWB food and placeable scripts on the same server.
