# Burger Shop

### 🍔 Burger Shop – Flip Patties, Run a Business

Open a fully playable burger restaurant on your FiveM server with crafting, stock management, billing, and boss menus. Burger Shop gives players a real job loop — from prepping food to ringing up customers and refilling stock.

Works with **ESX**, **QBCore**, and **Qbox** through FS Bridge.

#### ⚙️ Key Features

* **Full Restaurant Loop** — Craft burgers, manage stock, sell to customers, and handle billing.
* **Boss & Management Menus** — Set prices, refill stock, and control the business from in-game panels.
* **Target or Marker Mode** — Use ox_target zones or classic markers depending on your setup.
* **Auto Refill Options** — Schedule automatic stock top-ups so the shop never runs dry.
* **Admin Tools** — Permission-based admin commands for restocking and oversight.
