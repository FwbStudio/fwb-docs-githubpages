# Npc Medic

### 🚑 NPC Medic – Help When EMS Is Offline

Give downed players a fallback when no medics are on duty. NPC Medic dispatches an AI medic to revive players for a fee, with cooldowns, EMS-count restrictions, and society payouts — all configurable.

Works with **ESX**, **QBCore**, and **Qbox** through FS Bridge.

#### ⚙️ Key Features

* **AI Medic Dispatch** — Dead players call for help and an NPC medic travels to revive them.
* **EMS Restrictions** — Disable the command when enough real EMS are online to keep jobs valuable.
* **Revive Fees** — Charge configurable min/max prices, optionally paid into the ambulance society.
* **Self & Other Revive** — Let players call for themselves or allow bystanders to request help for others.
* **Blacklist Zones** — Block medic calls in hospitals, events, or custom areas.

#### 💼 Perfect For:

* Low-EMS population hours
* Hardcore servers that still need a safety net
* Roleplay servers with revive fees
* Ambulance job servers balancing realism and access
* Event hosts who want controlled revive access
