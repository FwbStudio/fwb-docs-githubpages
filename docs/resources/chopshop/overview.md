# ChopShop

### 🔧 ChopShop – Strip Cars, Stack Cash

Turn stolen rides into profit with a full vehicle chopping system for criminal and economy-focused FiveM servers. Players bring hot cars to your chop zones, break them down for parts, and sell the loot — adding real risk and reward to auto theft RP.

Supports **ESX**, **QBCore**, and **Qbox** via FS Bridge.

#### ⚙️ Key Features

* **Vehicle Chopping** — Break down vehicles at configured chop locations for parts and payouts.
* **Configurable Rewards** — Set which items or money players receive per vehicle type.
* **Job & Zone Control** — Restrict chopping to specific jobs, areas, or gang territories.
* **Inventory Ready** — Includes item setup guides for ox_inventory, qb-inventory, and more.
* **Bridge Compatible** — Works with FWB Bridge for framework, inventory, and target integration.

#### 💼 Perfect For:

* Gang and criminal RP
* Illegal side-hustle economies
* Auto theft storylines
* Servers wanting meaningful consequences for stolen cars
* Underground mechanic or scrapyard businesses
