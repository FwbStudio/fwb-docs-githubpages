# Carwipe

### 🚗 Carwipe – Keep Your Streets Clean

Keep abandoned vehicles under control with a powerful admin car wipe system built for busy FiveM servers. Whether you need a scheduled cleanup or an on-demand wipe before a major event, Carwipe gives staff full control over what gets removed and what stays protected.

Works with **ESX**, **QBCore**, and **Qbox** through FS Bridge.

#### ⚙️ Key Features

* **Scheduled Auto Wipe** — Run automatic vehicle cleanup on a timer you define in config.
* **Instant Admin Wipe** — Trigger a full wipe on demand with permission-based commands.
* **Whitelist Protection** — Exclude specific vehicle models and license plates from deletion.
* **Permanent & In-Game Zones** — Set protected areas where vehicles are never removed, and create temporary wipe zones in-game.
* **Broken-Only Mode** — Optionally delete only destroyed vehicles instead of every empty car on the map.
* **Flexible Permissions** — Restrict zone and wipe commands by admin group, job, ACE, identifier, or character.

#### 💼 Perfect For:

* High-population city servers
* Admin teams managing vehicle spam
* Event hosts clearing the map before races or scenes
* Police or staff-controlled cleanup RP
* Servers that need protected garage or dealership zones
